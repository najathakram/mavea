## Recommendation

**Zeenath** — and it isn't close:

1. `zeenath.lk` is free; every serious rival lost its domain or its reputation check.
2. It is an extremely common Sri Lankan Muslim woman's name — the brand reads as *ours* to the
   audience in a way an imported name never would. Proudly-SL positioning gets it for free.
3. It literally means **adornment** (زينة) — a fashion brand named "adornment" in her own
   naming tradition.
4. Collisions are negligible (a 223-follower account, a salon in a different category), and the
   Zeenat Aman echo is glamour, not baggage.
5. The "personal boutique" register is a styling problem, not a naming problem — a modern
   wordmark (clean type, no ornament) makes Zeenath read contemporary.

Runner-up if Najath vetoes: **Hayaa** (`hayaa.lk`) — the concept (modesty itself) is unbeatable
but the global field is crowded and the Sinhala "six" homophone is a permanent small tax.

`.com`/handle variants to verify at registration with the winner: `wearzeenath.com` /
`zeenath.store` / `zeenathofficial.com`; IG `@zeenath.lk` / `@wearzeenath` (bare `@zeenath`
assumed taken).

## Finalist collision scans

- **NILARA — MINOR, viable.** Collisions: a low-profile Malta apparel label (nilara.com.mt,
  has/had a USPTO apparel filing — irrelevant to SL-only commerce but check NIPO before
  filing); Nilara Inn (Nawalapitiya) + Yoho Nilara Resort (Ella) — hospitality, different
  category; small unrelated users (US coaching, Indian mineral water). Meanings confirmed:
  Tamil நிலா *nila* = moon (poetic, beloved); Sinhala නිල් *nil* = blue — as in **Nil Manel,
  the blue water lily, Sri Lanka's national flower**. No negative meaning in Arabic, Sinhala,
  Tamil, Hindi/Urdu. Rare genuine feminine name (moon/serene water).
- **IFFA — CROWDED, eliminated.** 3+ existing modest-fashion brands (Iffa Collection Pakistan,
  Iffa's Fashion Italy, Malaysian Iffa hijab brand + IFFAH US abayas); iffa.com is IFFA
  Frankfurt, the global **meat-industry trade fair**; English "iffy" mishearing risk.

## REVISED RECOMMENDATION (supersedes Round 1)

**NILARA** — the coined-track winner, and now the overall recommendation:

- **@nilara is FREE on Instagram** — the scarcest asset in this entire exploration, and the
  one Najath prioritized. (TikTok bare @nilara is a dormant 12-follower dead account → use
  `@nilara.lk`.)
- `nilara.lk` free · `wearnilara.com` free (bare .com is an unrelated coaching firm — fine).
- The story is native to the whole island: **moon in Tamil (nila), blue in Sinhala (nil, the
  national flower), and the crescent above both** — modest beauty as moonlight: luminous
  without glare. Sounds Arabic-adjacent (Nila/Naila family) so it reads naturally Muslim too.
- No negative meanings; collisions minor and out-of-category for SL.

**Real-name-track alternative: Zeenath** (`zeenath.lk` free, means "adornment", deeply local —
but bare @zeenath is a taken personal account, so IG would be `@zeenath.lk`).
