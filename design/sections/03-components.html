Glass recipe

Motion & touch
280ms cubic-bezier(.22,1,.36,1) throughout. Hover lifts 2–5px, press scales to .97. Radii 16 field / 20 tile / 24 panel / pill buttons. Targets 44px, 8px apart. All durations collapse to 0 under prefers-reduced-motion.
