EXCHANGE POLICY
AESHAL
Exchanges
We exchange, we do not refund. Here is exactly what that means, in plain words.
You have 7 days
Counted from the day it reaches you. Message us on WhatsApp and we start it there — no forms.
The courier collects
From your door, at a time you choose. We pay the return leg; you pay Rs. 350 towards sending the new size out.
Unworn, with the tag on
Try it on over your clothes. If it has been worn out, washed, altered or perfumed, we cannot exchange it.
Hijabs and undercaps
Only if the seal is unopened — hygiene, not fine print.
If we got it wrong
Wrong piece, a fault, or damage in transit — we collect it and send the right one, and you pay nothing at all.
Avoid the exchange entirely
Send us your bust and height before you order and we will measure the actual piece.
