PHOTOGRAPHY · SHOOT SPEC
Hand this to whoever shoots. The modesty rules are constraints, not preferences — and the crops are what make a full-length garment survive a 390px screen.
Crops, and what each one is for
3:4
Grid card. Head and hem both inside the frame — never crop either.
2:3
Hero and PDP. The whole silhouette is the product.
4:3
Detail. Weave, weight, hem finish, at close range.
1:1
Share card and Instagram. One per piece.
Non-negotiable in every frame
✓Hem to the ankle, sleeves to the wrist, high neckline
✓Hair fully covered
✓Loose, non-clinging drape; opaque fabric, never backlit through the cloth
✓No skin above the wrist or below the ankle
Look and setting
Daylight. Real Sri Lankan interiors and streets. Colour kept as shot — the site is neutral so the garment can be the only colour on screen. No studio seamless, no dunes, no skylines, no gold props.
Delivery to the site
AVIF with WebP fallback. Hero under 200KB. Width and height always declared. Everything below the fold lazy-loaded. Four images per piece: full-length, back, detail, worn-in-context.
